Stay close to Arunachalam, rest in comfort

Arunaramana Leela Tower is a clean, family friendly AC lodge in RS Nagar, near Ramanashram Post, Tiruvannamalai. It is built for pilgrims, devotees and families visiting Arunachalam Temple and the sacred hill.

Rooms are spacious and spotless, the building is safe and secure, and a lift and generator backup keep your stay easy at any hour.
